Nibras Terminal privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 · App version 1.0.0 (store)

1. Summary

Nibras Terminal is a Linux terminal for Android. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking library. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your files, commands and keys stay on your device or in a place you choose.

2. Information we do not collect

The developer does not collect or receive your name, e-mail, device identifiers, analytics data, terminal history, or the contents of your files. The app contains no advertising, Firebase, Play Services or analytics SDKs.

3. Data stored on your device

Settings, SSH hosts and keys, scripts, installed Linux environments and terminal history are kept in the app's own storage. The store version does not ask for access to all files: shared files are kept in the app folder (~/storage). This data does not leave the device unless a command you type sends it.

4. Network

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to: to download Linux distributions and packages from official mirrors and GitHub, and for services you start yourself, such as SSH, git and downloads. Those servers see your IP address, as with any download.

5. Optional online features

Prayer times send the city you choose to api.aladhan.com; Quran text is loaded from api.alquran.cloud. Nothing else about you is sent. If you do not use these features, those requests are not made.

6. Permissions

Camera, microphone, location, contacts, placing a call, showing notifications and similar access are requested only when you run a command that needs them, not when the app opens. The result is shown only in your terminal and is not sent anywhere unless your own command sends it. Prayer reminders and widgets may ask for permission to show notifications.

7. What this store version does not include

The store version does not include reading or sending SMS, the call log, access to all files, one-tap APK install, a notification-reading service, a floating window, or the Kali/NetHunter environment. The full-featured build remains on GitHub. This app is not affiliated with the Alpine, Debian, Ubuntu, Arch or Fedora projects.

8. Use from a computer

The built-in remote terminal works only on your local Wi-Fi, uses a one-time link and stops by itself. Traffic does not go through the developer's servers.

9. Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash.txt file is saved on your device. It is shared only if you send it yourself.

10. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It is a tool and is not directed at children under 13.

11. Deletion

Uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → Nibras Terminal → Storage, to remove all of its data. Because we hold nothing on any server, there is nothing for us to delete remotely.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date and publish the new version on this page.

13. Contact

Questions: nibrascode@gmail.com, or https://github.com/nibrascode/nibras-terminal/issues.
