Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Mushafuk.online is for reading the Madinah mushaf page by page.

The site is published by Nibras Code.

What we do not collect

There is no account.

We do not take your name, email, phone, location, contacts, or device id.

There are no ads.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Site statistics

On mushafuk.online the site records basic visit statistics: the page path, the referring website (if any), the page language, and whether the screen looks like a phone or a computer.

If you use search, the search text (up to 100 characters) and the number of results may also be recorded, so the site can be improved.

This is sent to our own database, hosted by Supabase. It is not used to build a profile of the reader, and it is not used for ads.

As with any website request, the server may see your IP address. We do not use it to identify you.

The Android app does not include this visit recording.

What stays on the device

Language, text size, and night mode stay on this device.

The tafsir, translation, i‘rab, and gharib book you pick stay on this device.

Bookmarks and notes stay on this device only.

They are not sent to a server.

Search

Search looks only at the Quran text, translations, and tafsirs that are on this site.

If the question is in Azerbaijani, Turkish, English, or Russian, the answer comes from that language’s translation.

The question is not sent to an outside model.

Internet and recitation

Internet is used to open a page, a tafsir, a translation, i‘rab, rare words, and the audio.

Audio opens through the EveryAyah service.

The connection is HTTPS.

This connection is not used to build a profile of the reader.

Mushafuk Android app

The Tasheeh (recitation check) feature in the app uses the microphone only when you press the mic button.

Speech is recognised on the phone itself; audio is never uploaded and never stored. Listening ends when you stop the mic. You can revoke the permission in your phone settings at any time.

The speech model (about 69 MB) is downloaded once over HTTPS, only when you ask for it, from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), or from quran.mushafuk.online as a fallback, and can be deleted in the app under Settings → Hifz.

When you download tafsir or translations, files are fetched over HTTPS from nihomaho9-ui.github.io. The Madinah page fonts (about 95 MB) are downloaded once over HTTPS from jsDelivr or GitHub, then kept on the device so the app can work offline.

Only standard technical data (such as an IP address) is visible to those hosts; we do not receive it, and nothing about you is sent.

The last page, bookmarks, settings, downloaded tafsir and translations, recitation-check history, and the speech model stay only on the device.

The app asks for microphone permission only when you first turn the mic on in Tasheeh mode. It does not request camera, location, contacts or file access.

The app has no account, and no ads or analytics.

Children

The site is for all ages. We do not knowingly collect names or contact details from children.

The content is the Quran, and there are no ads.
