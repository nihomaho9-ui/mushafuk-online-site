Who we are

Mushafuk.Online is an independent personal initiative created to make reading and understanding the Qur’an easier.

The project is developed as a personal initiative by a student of the Islamic University of Madinah.

The platform’s main aim is to present the Qur’an, meaning-based translations in various languages, and reliable tafsir sources in one simple, easy-to-use place.
