EveryAyah

Verses are played from the EveryAyah service.

The reciters are Maher Al Muaiqly, Husary, Nasser Al Qatami, Yasser Al Dosari, Muhammad Ayyub, Muhammad Jibreel, and Saud Ash-Shuraim.

These recordings are not inside the mushaf permission.

The rights stay with the reciter and with the party that publishes the files.

If a rightsholder wants a text corrected or removed, the work is named here so that its source is visible.
