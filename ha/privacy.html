Kididdigar shafin

A mushafuk.online shafin yana rubuta ƙididdigar ziyara ta asali: hanyar shafin, shafin da kuka fito daga gare shi (idan akwai), harshen shafin, da kuma ko allon ya yi kama da waya ko kwamfuta.

Idan kun yi amfani da bincike, rubutun bincike (har zuwa haruffa 100) da adadin sakamakon kuma ana iya rubuta su, don a inganta shafin.

Ana aika wannan zuwa tattara bayananmu, wanda Supabase ke ɗaukar nauyin sa. Ba a yi amfani da shi wajen gina bayanin mai karatu ba, kuma ba a yi amfani da shi don talla ba.

Kamar kowace buƙatar yanar gizo, uwar garke na iya ganin adireshin IP ɗinku. Ba mu yi amfani da shi don gane ku ba.

Manhajar Android ba ta ƙunshi wannan rubutun ziyara ba.

Abin da yake a kan na’ura

Harshe, girman rubutu, da yanayin dare suna a kan wannan na’ura.

Tafsiri, fassara, i‘rab, da littafin gharib da kuka zaɓa suna a kan wannan na’ura.

Alamomin shafi da bayanai suna a kan wannan na’ura kawai.

Ba a aika su zuwa uwar garke ba.

Bincike

Bincike yana kallo kawai ga rubutun Alkur'ani, fassarori, da tafsirai da ke a wannan shafi.

Idan tambayar tana cikin Azerbaijanci, Turki, Ingilishi, ko Rashanci, amsa tana zuwa daga fassarar wannan harshe.

Ba a aika tambayar zuwa wani samfurin waje ba.

Intanet da karatu

Ana amfani da intanet don buɗe shafi, tafsiri, fassara, i‘rab, kalmomi da baƙi, da sauti.

Sauti yana buɗewa ta hidimar EveryAyah.

Haɗin yana tafiya da HTTPS.

Ba a yi amfani da wannan haɗin don gina bayanin mai karatu ba.

Manhajar Android ta Mushafuk

Siffar Tasheeh (duba karatun) a cikin manhaja tana amfani da makirufo kawai lokacin da kuka danna maɓallin makirufo.

Ana gane magana a kan waya kanta; ba a taba loda sauti ba kuma ba a adana shi ba. Sauraro yana ƙarewa da kuka tsaya makirufo. Kuna iya janye izinin a kowane lokaci a cikin saitunan waya.

Samfurin magana (kusan MB 69) ana sauke shi sau ɗaya ta HTTPS, kawai idan kuka nema, daga GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), ko daga quran.mushafuk.online a matsayin madadin, kuma ana iya goge shi a manhaja a ƙarƙashin Saituna → Hifz.

Idan kuka sauke tafsiri ko fassarori, ana ɗaukar fayiloli ta HTTPS daga nihomaho9-ui.github.io. Rubutun shafukan Madina (kusan MB 95) ana sauke su sau ɗaya ta HTTPS daga jsDelivr ko GitHub, sai a ajiye su a na’ura don manhaja ta yi aiki ba tare da intanet ba.

Bayanan fasaha na yau da kullum kawai (kamar adireshin IP) ke bayyana wa waɗannan rundunonin; ba mu karɓe su ba, kuma ba a aika komai game da ku ba.

Shafin ƙarshe, alamomin shafi, saituna, tafsirin da fassarorin da aka sauke, tarihin duba karatun, da samfurin magana suna a kan na’ura kawai.

Manhaja tana neman izinin makirufo kawai lokacin da kuka kunna makirufo a karon farko a yanayin Tasheeh. Ba ta neman kyamara, wuri, lambobin sadarwa, ko shiga fayiloli ba.

Manhaja ba ta da asusu, kuma babu talla ko nazari.
